PROJECT CASE STUDY / POWER BI · HEALTHCARE

Hospital
Analytics Dashboard

An interactive healthcare dashboard exploring encounter volume, patient flow, department workload, length of stay and discharge turnaround.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

What question
does it answer?

The goal is to make hospital operations easier to understand through a consolidated view of key service indicators. The analysis areas include encounter volume over time, patient flow, department workload, length of stay and discharge turnaround.

APPROACH

From data to
understanding.

A dashboard case study should show the source data, cleaning steps, measures, visuals and the decisions the findings can support.
